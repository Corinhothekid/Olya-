# Ultimate Team player catalog

The match roster and economy draw from 46 original playable athletes. Every card has a fictional development rating and a field position. All three collection affiliations contain Elite, High, and Core cards. The eight legacy card IDs retain their ratings and use original athlete names to preserve ownership during migration.

The editorial catalog contains 65 independently sourced records. Season, club, and field or box positions follow the cited record. Hall of Fame entries can omit a season or club. A separate research queue retains unresolved entries. Six original home and away colorways are available in the Jersey Vault.

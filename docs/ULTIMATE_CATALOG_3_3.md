# Ultimate Team player catalog

The playable pool contains 46 original athletes and 72 reviewed real-player editions. The catalog holds 95 sourced records and 42 unresolved research entries. All eight legacy card IDs retain their original names and ratings so existing collections load without conversion.

Each reviewed edition identifies its league, season, club, position, and evidence. Different league and season cards have different edition IDs. A shared player identity lets the starting lineup treat those editions as the same athlete. Tom Schreiber, for example, has 2022 PLL, 2017 MLL, and 2017 NLL cards.

Real-player cards display factual names with original artwork. The Jersey Vault retains six original home and away colorways. League badges, photographs, and replica uniforms are absent. The project is unaffiliated.

## Ratings and field roles

Ratings are fictional game values. For a reviewed edition, the starting rating reflects its documented evidence, such as a regular season line, season honors, or an award. Role-specific measures add up to five rating points. Scoring, caused turnovers, ground or loose balls, faceoff percentage, and save percentage have different thresholds for their roles and playing formats.

On-field attributes start from PolyLax's position profiles and get small edition bonuses. An assists-heavy attack card gets more passing emphasis. A leading faceoff specialist gets stronger faceoff and ground-ball attributes, and a goalie with a high save percentage gets a reflex bonus. NLL forwards and transition players keep their source positions on their cards and fill attack and midfield slots in the 10v10 field game.

Regular pack and wheel draws use the published 20% Elite, 35% High, and 45% Core odds. Adding editions changes the selection inside a rarity tier. Only editions explicitly reviewed against the listed evidence can enter the pools. Hall of Fame identities without a verified dated season stay in the reference catalog.

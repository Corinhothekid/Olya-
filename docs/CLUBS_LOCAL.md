# PolyLax Clubs local milestone

The main menu now has Quick Play, Be a Pro, PolyLax Clubs, and Settings. Quick Play retains Practice and Full Match. The original standalone Player Creator is in Settings. Be a Pro uses its existing career save and menus.

Clubs uses its own local save. Create a local club with a high-school, college, or professional team, preview its 10-player roster and league schedule, customize a separate athlete in the existing 3D creator, and spend points in Player Development. The lobby waits for an online relay before displaying an invitation. This milestone does not create online rooms.

To test in Codea, compare the name and position in Be a Pro with My Player in Clubs, customize a Clubs athlete, create a club, check its uniforms and schedule, and spend a starter skill point. Close and reopen PolyLax and verify both saves. Lua tests with mocked Craft cover these paths and the original Quick Play and Be a Pro screens. Native Codea testing is pending.
